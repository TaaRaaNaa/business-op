# Product FAQ (Demo)

**Q: What products are in the FlexOps demo catalog?**
A: FlexOMS (order management), FlexEMS (execution management), FlexAlgo Suite (algo trading tools),
FlexAnalytics (post-trade analytics), and FlexConnect API (integration layer for OMS/EMS connectivity).

**Q: What counts as a "Strategic" tier account?**
A: Accounts with ARR above $300,000 or accounts flagged by leadership as top-20 relationships regardless of ARR.

**Q: How is renewal risk assessed?**
A: A composite of (a) usage trend over trailing 90 days, (b) open High-severity feedback items,
and (c) days since last executive touchpoint (QBR or exec call).

**Q: What's the standard onboarding timeline?**
A: Target is 6 weeks from contract signature to go-live for Mid-Market, 10 weeks for Enterprise/Strategic
given deeper OMS/EMS integration work.
