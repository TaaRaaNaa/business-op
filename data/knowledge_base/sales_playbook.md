# FlexOps Sales Playbook (Demo)

## Opportunity Stages
1. **Prospecting** — Initial outreach, no confirmed interest yet.
2. **Qualification** — BANT confirmed (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline).
3. **Needs Analysis** — Discovery call(s) completed, pain points documented in CRM.
4. **Proposal Sent** — Pricing and SOW shared; reps must log proposal value and expected close date.
5. **Negotiation** — Legal/procurement engaged; CLM (contract lifecycle management) tracked in LinkSquares-equivalent.
6. **Closed Won / Closed Lost** — Loss reason is mandatory for all Closed Lost opportunities.

## Lead Leakage Policy
An opportunity is flagged as "at risk of leakage" if:
- No activity logged in 21+ days, AND
- Stage is Qualification through Negotiation (not yet closed)

Reps are notified automatically; if no action within 5 business days, the deal escalates to the regional Sales Ops lead for review.

## Escalation Thresholds
- Deals over $200,000 ARR require VP Sales sign-off before moving to Negotiation.
- Renewal opportunities at risk (churn signal) must be flagged to Client Services within 48 hours of detection.

## Quarterly Business Review (QBR) Cadence
Strategic and Enterprise tier accounts receive a QBR every quarter. Mid-Market accounts receive a QBR every two quarters unless flagged high-risk.
